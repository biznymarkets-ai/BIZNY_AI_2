8. DEAL AGREEMENT FLOW

A deal should not become active immediately.

Flow:

1. User expresses interest or responds to request/opportunity.
2. Deal draft is created.
3. Both parties review terms.
4. Co-Pilot summarizes the deal.
5. Parties edit or accept terms.
6. Witnesses or third parties are added if required.
7. Field agent verification is requested if physical location, asset, business, project, or milestone is involved.
8. Both parties click Agree.
9. Deal becomes Active.
10. Milestones are tracked.
11. Updates and evidence are uploaded.
12. Deal is completed, cancelled, or disputed.

Agreement button:

Each party must click:

I have reviewed and agree to these terms.

Show timestamp and user identity.
