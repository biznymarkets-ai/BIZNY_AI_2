## Specific Recommendations for the Website

### 1. Lead With the Problem, Not the Platform

**Current:**

> "Build Real Ventures Across Africa. Discover opportunities, follow proven templates, find trusted collaborators..."

**Recommended:**

> **"Africa has everything it needs to prosper. Except coordination."**
>
> You have the skills. The land. The ideas. The drive.
> But finding the right partner, the right supplier, the right buyer — and knowing you can trust them — costs more time and money than most people can afford.
>
> **Bizny fixes that.**

This immediately signals: *This is about you and your problem, not about our platform.*

## The One-Sentence Test

Can a cassava farmer in Oyo State, a diaspora investor in London, and a logistics provider in Accra all read the homepage and immediately understand **what problem Bizny solves for them personally**?

**Current answer:** No.

**Target answer:** Yes — and within 10 seconds, they should know which button to click.

---

## Final Recommendation

The website should be rebuilt around **three user journeys**, each with:

1. **The specific pain** (what hurts right now)
2. **The specific promise** (what changes with Bizny)
3. **The specific proof** (one real person, one real result)
4. **The specific action** (one clear button to click)

Everything else — the systems theory, the architecture, the philosophy, the "economic node" language — belongs in the **About page**, the **white paper**, or the **investor deck**. Not the homepage.

The homepage's only job is to make the right person feel:

> **"This is for me. This solves my problem. I need to try this."**
