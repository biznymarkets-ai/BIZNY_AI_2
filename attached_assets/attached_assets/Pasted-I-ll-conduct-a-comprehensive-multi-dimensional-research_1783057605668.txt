# Dimension 1: Product-Market Fit

## The Problem Is Real and Quantified

### The SME Finance Gap

Africa's SME financing gap is estimated at **$331 billion** (sub-Saharan Africa) to **$421 billion** (full continent). Only approximately 20% of African SMEs have access to formal financing. Collateral requirements average **181% of loan value**, far above the global 120% average.

### The Missing Middle

African SMEs are too big for microfinance and too small for commercial banks.

This is not primarily a capital shortage.

It is a **coordination and information shortage**.

Banks cannot assess risk because there is no reliable data on SME operations, productive capacity, execution history, or trustworthiness.

### The Trust Deficit

A 2026 analysis documents that **$31 billion** in import transactions in Ghana alone resulted in no goods ever arriving.

Only 10,440 of those transactions were linked to actual imports.

The remainder represent coordination failures involving fraud, misinformation, broken supply chains, and verification failures.

### Informal Cross-Border Trade Costs

Informal traders pay approximately **62% more per ton** than formal traders to move goods across borders because they lack coordination infrastructure.

They lack:

* trust
* documentation
* verification
* information
* standardized processes

### AfCFTA Amplifies The Need

The African Continental Free Trade Area creates a **$3.4 trillion** market serving **1.4 billion people**.

However, implementation continues to be constrained by:

* inadequate infrastructure
* inconsistent regulations
* non-tariff barriers

The East African Community Single Customs Territory demonstrated what coordinated infrastructure can achieve.

Transport times fell from **21 days to 7 days**.

Shipment costs fell from **$3,100 to $1,025**.

This required integrated digital coordination systems.

Product-Market Fit Score:

**9.0 / 10**

The demand is measurable.

The need is structural.

The problem is worsening rather than disappearing.
