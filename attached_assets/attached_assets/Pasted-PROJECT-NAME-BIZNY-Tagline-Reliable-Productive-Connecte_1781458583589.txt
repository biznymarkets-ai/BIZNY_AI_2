PROJECT NAME

MISSION

Bizny is a productivity and coordination platform designed to help people discover opportunities, connect with collaborators, verify information, coordinate resources, and build productive ventures across Africa.

Bizny is NOT a social media platform.

Bizny is NOT a fintech.

Bizny is NOT an e-commerce checkout platform.

Bizny is a productivity network and venture coordination platform.

Its purpose is to connect:

People
Skills
Capital
Knowledge
Resources
Opportunities

and help transform them into productive ventures.
